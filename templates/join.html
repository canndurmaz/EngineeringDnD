What the six stats mean
RIGOR
Analysis, math, verification
INTUITION
Debugging instinct, pattern recognition
CRAFT
Fabrication, soldering, rework, bench work
SYSTEMS
Architecture, interfaces, whole-system reasoning
COMMS
Stakeholders, documentation, persuasion
GRIT
Endurance, stress tolerance
  • Every action rolls d20 + stat modifier against a difficulty.
  • A modifier is (score − 10) ÷ 2, rounded down.
  • Stamina = 8 + GRIT mod + 2 × level — the stress you absorb before burning out.
  • Focus = 4 + the better of RIGOR and SYSTEMS mods — your budget for abilities.
  • Your stats are rolled 4d6, dropping the lowest die, six times. Your class’s primary and secondary stats always receive your two best rolls.
  • Each stat is then clamped to the 8–16 range — so a very low or very high roll is pulled back toward the middle.
